# A guided visit to Hearthafter

Choose the [quick video](https://hearthafter.homes/about#quick-demo) or follow this interactive route. The [live guide](https://hearthafter.homes/about#deep-dive) keeps the same route close to the application.

## 1. Meet someone before considering a match

[Meet Orin Pell](https://hearthafter.homes/spirits/spirit_orin_pell). Read his history, company preferences and camera-free boundary. These are linked published Sanity records, not isolated profile copy.

## 2. See a boundary stop a recommendation

[Begin a household application](https://hearthafter.homes/find), choose June and Leila's example and select **See its possible placements now**. Open [Iona and Orin's comparison](https://hearthafter.homes/match/spirit_iona_vale~spirit_orin_pell).

Use **Compare a household** to select Noor's home. **A boundary comes first** explains why Noor's active indoor camera conflicts with Orin's camera-free presence area. The comparison is excluded before preference scoring. Choosing **Use this household** does not remove that boundary or enable a review.

## 3. Negotiate a preference without overriding a boundary

Use **Compare a household** to choose June and Leila's camera-free home, then select **Use this household**. Read **A quieter kind of midnight** and select its checkbox. The comparison changes from 76 to 90 preference points and **Review an introduction** becomes available. This changes the quiet-hours comparison only. It does not grant permission for a camera in Noor's home.

Open the review to inspect the plan, outstanding questions and separate consent decisions. The plan and required introduction answers come before agreement. Refusal remains available. A browser reload preserves your case.

## 4. Follow the records behind a decision

[Explore the linked registry](https://hearthafter.homes/about#behind-the-service). Select Orin in **Registered spirit** to see the history, traits, boundaries and relationship references loaded from Sanity.

[Read the verified change-impact example](https://hearthafter.homes/about#source-change). An earlier authenticated test created a real Sanity draft, published a changed camera policy and restored the original facts. The draft remained private. Publishing blocked the match and invalidated the earlier case approval. Restoring the facts did not restore the old consent, because the source revision had changed.

That section reports a completed test. It is not an interactive replay or a live administrator edit. The [controlled drill documentation](change-impact-drill.md) explains its scope and limits. The signed-in App SDK editor was not exercised by that drill.

## 5. Keep the family together

Return to [the application](https://hearthafter.homes/find) and choose **Robin & Esme's small-garden house**, which offers three departed places. Review [Kit, Leon and Mara together](https://hearthafter.homes/match/spirit_kit_aster~spirit_leon_aster~spirit_mara_aster).

Their required-companion references keep the family intact. Kit remains with Mara, the documented guardian. Open a review and inspect all five pending decisions: the living household, Leon, Mara, Mara's care agreement for Kit and Kit's separate age-appropriate assent. A child's refusal cannot be replaced by an adult's agreement.

For an adult trio, choose **The Lantern House student share** and inspect [Iona, Orin and Tavi](https://hearthafter.homes/match/spirit_iona_vale~spirit_orin_pell~spirit_tavi_mercer). Read the quiet-hours arrangement and every resident's and relationship's explanation. A household offering two places cannot silently expand to fit three.

## What persists, and where

The public registry is backed by Sanity project `o3jy1zm6`, dataset `production`: nine spirits, five homes and linked source documents. Visitor answers, reviews and their simulated audit timeline persist only in that browser. They do not create private staff cases or contact anyone.

Authorized staff use Sanity sign-in, the App SDK desk and native Workflows. Those paths are separate from the anonymous visitor demonstration. A failed live read shows a retry state and never silently switches to bundled fixtures.
